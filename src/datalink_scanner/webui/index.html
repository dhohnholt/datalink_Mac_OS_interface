What is sent

Only the scored report for the session you choose.

  • Student IDs, names, per-question responses and scores for that test.
  • Per-question statistics and the class summary.
  • Sent over HTTPS to .
  • Your token travels only as an authorization header, never in the report body, and is never logged.
  • No password, email, or other account credential is requested or transmitted.
